Where these snapshots come from

Each line of history.ndjson is one read of Roblox's Top Trending chart (and, where it was read, its Top Playing Now
chart and the trending games' games/v1 details), as its "source" names:

- makefun/roblox-chart-recorder: make.fun's own recorder (collector/roblox/record.ts in the make.fun repository), which reads
  https://apis.roblox.com/explore-api/v1/get-sort-content?sortId=top-trending&sessionId=<uuid>&device=computer&country=us
  on every whole UTC hour, then sortId=top-playing-now, then https://games.roblox.com/v1/games for the trending games.
- kenny-dhub/roblox-snapshots@<commit>: the public archive https://github.com/Kenny-Dhub/roblox-snapshots, its
  data/discover/latest.json at that commit (a GitHub Actions read of Roblox's chart sorts, roughly every 4 to 8
  hours from 2026-10-30), its rank and player count per chart, converted line by line by collector/roblox/freeze.ts
  archiveSnapshot. It is used under its MIT licence:
